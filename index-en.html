How to choose

Marble or granite?

Marble

It offers elegant veining and a refined appearance. It is often preferred for interiors, bathrooms, tables and decorative features.

Granite

It is highly resistant to wear and everyday use. A practical choice for countertops, stairs, flooring and outdoor spaces.
